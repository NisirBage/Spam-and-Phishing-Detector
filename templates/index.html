Spam & Phishing Detector

Two ML models trained on real-world data: a TF-IDF + Logistic Regression text classifier for email/SMS spam, and a Random Forest over engineered lexical features for phishing URLs. Everything runs locally — no message or link you submit is sent anywhere else.
